About Us

A living palace, four centuries in the making.

Once the residence of a Rajput dynasty, Luxoria has welcomed guests as royalty since 1887. Behind its hand-carved façades lie frescoed halls, mirrored durbar rooms, and courtyards where fountains still sing at dusk — restored, never replaced, so every stay is a walk through living history.
